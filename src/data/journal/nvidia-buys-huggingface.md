Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for nearly $13 billion, bringing one of the most important open AI platforms under the same company that already dominates the hardware powering much of the AI industry. [Business Matters](https://bmmagazine.co.uk/ai/nvidia-hugging-face-deal-open-ai-models/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Hugging Face has become one of the main places where developers share, test and build with open AI models. It sits somewhere between GitHub and an app store for machine learning, which makes the acquisition particularly interesting.

For Nvidia, this goes beyond selling GPUs. The company has been steadily expanding further into the software and infrastructure around AI, and owning Hugging Face gives it a much stronger position in the part of the industry where models are actually distributed and used. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/podcasts/nepal-rescue-nvidias-bet-texas-vs-big-tech-2026-09-04/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The more interesting question is what happens to Hugging Face itself.

Its appeal has always been that it feels relatively open and independent, especially compared with the increasingly closed ecosystems surrounding many large AI companies. Putting it under Nvidia could give the platform far more resources, but it also means yet another important part of the AI ecosystem is being absorbed by one of the industry’s biggest players.

For now, Hugging Face is still Hugging Face. But the acquisition is another reminder that the AI industry is moving very quickly from experimentation into consolidation.

And Nvidia clearly does not want to be known as just the company making the chips anymore
